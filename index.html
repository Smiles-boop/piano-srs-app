      Welcome

      Load a MIDI file of a piano piece to get started. PianoSRS splits it into short practice sections automatically and shows the notes falling Synthesia-style over a keyboard. Connect a MIDI keyboard (or use the on-screen / computer keys) and play each section cleanly 10 times in a row to complete a review.

      Pieces you add are saved locally in your browser (IndexedDB) and reload automatically on your next visit. Note detection uses the Web MIDI API — best in Chrome or Edge.
